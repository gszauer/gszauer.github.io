Physics Architecture Guide

Spout-based level with dual water emitters.

1. Spout System

  • Emitters: Left and right wall spouts continuously spawn particles with directional velocity.
  • Flow Rate: Configurable emission rate, capped by max particle count.
  • Visuals: Pipe nozzles with valve wheels on the terrain layer.

2. Fluid Dynamics (SPH)

  • Spatial Hashing: O(n) neighbor lookup for pressure/viscosity.
  • Anti-Stacking Shear: Micro jitter prevents rigid vertical lattices.

3. Destructible Terrain

  • Grid State: 2D Byte Array (0=Empty, 1=Dirt, 2=Rock).
  • Carving: Interpolated circle stamps remove dirt on drag.
