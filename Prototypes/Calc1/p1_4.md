# Level select

Since this is more than 8 levels, our level selector will not work. Instead, when the player hits the options button it should look like this:

+-----+-----+-----+
| EMP | EMP | EMP |
+-----+-----+-----+
|  -  | NUM |  +  |
+-----+-----+-----+
| OPT | EMP | EMP |
+-----+-----+-----+

In the middle is a number, to it's left are buttons to increase it and decrease it. When the player clicks opt again, this either closes to the current menu, or if the number ins't the same, starts the selected level.
