How to Play Boxelot

- Start by clicking tiles with a '?' adjacent (Up/Down/Left/Right) to revealed areas.
- Tiles without symbols are currently unreachable.
- Tiles with 'X' are blocked by adjacent revealed monsters.
- MONSTER (Icon on Red Bg): Click again to attack. Combat is turn-based. Defeat monsters to unblock adjacent tiles.
- KEY HOLDER: Attack the monster! If it holds the key, a 🗝️ icon will appear next to it after your first hit.
- KEY ITEM (🗝️ on Yellow Bg): Click the dropped key (after defeating the key holder) to pick it up.
- DOOR (Brown Bg): Initially revealed. Shows locked (🔒) or unlocked (🔓). Click the unlocked door WITH THE KEY to advance to the next floor (this consumes the key).
- GOLD (Yellow Bg): Click revealed gold to collect.
- TRAP (Dark Grey Bg): Damages you instantly upon reveal.
- ITEM (Icon on Green Bg): Click the revealed item icon to pick it up.
- INVENTORY (Footer): Click items (max 6). A popup appears showing item details (like 🍷5 for a potion healing 5 HP). Click 'Use' to activate or 'Cancel'.
- EMPTY (Grey Bg): Clears the tile, potentially revealing adjacent '?'.
- Goal: Reach the deepest floor possible!
