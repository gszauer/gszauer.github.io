How to Play Boxelot

The Goal

Descend as deep into the dungeon as possible! Find the key 🗝️ on each floor, unlock the door 🔓, and proceed to the next level.

Basics

  • Reveal Tiles: Click tiles marked with '?' to reveal tiles. Only tiles adjacent to already revealed tiles can be revealed.
  • Unreachable Tiles: Tiles without '?' or 'X' cannot be clicked yet.
  • Blocked Tiles: Tiles marked 'X' are blocked by an adjacent, revealed monster. Defeat the monster to reveal the tile.
  • Floor Clear Bonus: Defeat all monsters on a floor before opening the door to receive a bonus!

Player & Inventory

  • Stats: Track your Health, Attack, and Gold in the header.
  • Inventory: The footer shows your inventory. Click an item to see details.
  • Using Items: For 'Usable' items, click 'Use' in the item detail panel to activate its effect.
  • Selling Items: Click 'Sell' in the item detail panel to gain Gold. All inventory items can be sold.

Item Drops

These items have an immediate effect when you interact with them on the grid. They are not added to your inventory.

  • Coin (🪙): Pick up to gain gold.
  • Heart (💖): Pick up to heal.
  • Explosive (🧨): Instantly deals 2 damage to YOU!

Usable Items

These items go into your inventory. Click an item in the inventory, then click 'Use' or 'Sell'.

  • Potion (🍷): Use to heal.
  • Blade Scroll (🗡️): Use to deal 3 damage to all revealed monsters.
  • Poison Vial (🧪): Use to poison all revealed monsters.
  • Freeze Scroll (❄️): Use to freeze all revealed monsters for 3 turns.
  • Sheep Scroll (🐑): Use to turn all revealed monsters into weak sheep.
  • Lock Pick (🧷): Use to open the floor door without needing the key 🗝️.

Durable Items

These items provide passive effects while held in your inventory. Click them in the inventory to view details and Sell. Only one of each durable item exists

  • Attack Rune (👊): Grants +10 Attack.
  • Defense Rune (✋): Grants +10 Maximum HP.
  • Shield (🛡️): Grants a 10% chance to dodge incoming monster attacks.
  • Trap (🪤): Monsters take 2 damage when they are first revealed.
  • Lantern (💡): Reveals the location of the hidden key 🗝️ on every floor.
  • Cloak (🧥): Prevents revealed monsters from blocking adjacent hidden tiles.
  • Vampire Ring (💍): Heals you for 1-3 HP whenever you deal damage to a monster.
  • Amulet of Greed (📿): Gain 1 Gold for every 3 HP lost from a single source of damage.
  • Cursed Rune (🔮): Reduces your Attack by 50%, but increases your Max HP by double the amount of Attack lost.

Monsters

Various monsters appear with increasing HP/ATK on deeper floors. Click a revealed monster to attack it. Combat is turn-based. You attack first (usually!), and if the enemy survives they attack back.

General Monsters: General monsters (🕷️🐺👹🐦‍🔥🦇💀🧟👿👺) will only attack you if you attack them. Defeated monsters have a chance to drop a Coin 🪙, and will unblock adjacent tiles. One regular monster on each floor holds the Key Item 🗝️ needed for the door.

Skilled Monsters: Skilled monsters act like regular monsters, but they have special abilities:

  • Thief (🥷): Steals Gold when revealed! Defeat it to get the gold back, plus extra.
  • Assassin (👤): Attacks YOU for 1-6 damage immediately upon being revealed!
  • Dark Healer (🧙‍♂️): Tries to heal other revealed monsters each turn.
  • Necromancer (🧛‍♂️): May summon Skeletons (💀) onto empty revealed tiles.
  • Evil Archer (🧝🏼): Attacks you after *every* action you take while it's revealed!

Shapeshifters: When these monsters HP reaches 0, they transform into a different form. You must defeat the second form to fully clear them.

  • Shellshifter (🐌->🐚): Basic shapeshifter.
  • Phoenix (🐦‍🔥->🦅): A fiery bird that reforms into an avian.

Bosses: Bosses are much tougher than regular monsters! Defeating a Boss guarantees a Durable item reward or a lot of gold.

  • Orc (🧌), Ghost (👻), Squid (🦑): Powerful bosses with high stats.
  • Lizard (🦕): A shapeshifting boss (see above). The reward is only granted after defeating its second (🐉) form.
  • Master Thief (🦹): Only appears if you possess valuable Durable items. Steals one when revealed! Defeat it to recover your stolen item.

Monster Status Effects

Monsters can be Poisoned (🧪 take damage each turn) or Frozen (❄️ skip their attack). Turning a monster into a Sheep (🐑) makes it much weaker.

Special Tiles

  • Door (🚪): Starts revealed. Unlocks 🔓 if you have the key 🗝️. Click the unlocked door to advance to the next floor.
  • Key (🗝️): Appears on the tile after defeating the Key Holder monster. Click the key to pick it up.
  • Barrel (🏺): Click a revealed barrel to break it open. It might contain an item, a monster, or nothing.
  • Random (✨): When revealed, rapidly cycles through potential items (🍷🗡️🧪❄️🐑💖🪙🧨). Click it again to stop the cycle and receive the currently displayed item.
  • Shop (🏪): Appears every few floors. Click to spend Gold 🪙 to increase Max HP, Attack, or fully heal.
  • Challenge (🎯): Appears every few floors. Interacting with it requires meeting certain conditions (like having enough HP ❤️). They may trigger automatically when specific events happen. Completing the challenge rewards a Durable item or Gold.
