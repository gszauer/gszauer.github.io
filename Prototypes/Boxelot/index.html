How to Play Boxelot

- Start by clicking tiles with a '?' adjacent (Up/Down/Left/Right) to revealed areas.
- Tiles without symbols ('') or with 'X' are blocked or unreachable.
- MONSTER (Icon on Red Bg): Click revealed monster to attack. Combat is turn-based (Player hits first by default). Shows statuses (🥦, ❄️). Defeat monsters to unblock neighbors & drop a Coin 🪙.
- KEY HOLDER: Attack the monster! A 🗝️ icon appears after first hit. Defeat it to drop the Key Item.
- KEY ITEM (🗝️ on Yellow Bg): Click the dropped key to pick it up.
- DOOR (🔒/🔓 on Brown Bg): Click unlocked door 🔓 with the key 🗝️ to advance. If floor clear (no monsters left), get a bonus!
- COIN (🪙 on Yellow Bg): Click revealed/dropped coin for +10 Gold.
- HEART (💖 on Pink Bg): Click revealed/dropped heart to heal 3-5 HP (only if not full health).
- TRAP (Dark Grey Bg): Damages you instantly upon reveal.
- BARREL (🏺 on Brown Bg): Click revealed barrel to break (Enemy? Coin? Potion? Heart? Key? Nothing?).
- RANDOM TILE (✨ on Grey Bg): Cycles items (🍷, 🗡️, 🥦, ❄️, 🐑, 💖, 🪙, 🧨). Click to get current or take damage from 🧨.
- ITEM (Icon on Green Bg): Click revealed item icon (🍷, 🗡️, 🥦, ❄️, 🐑) to pick it up.
- INVENTORY (Footer): Click items (max 6). A popup appears showing item details (like 🍷5 for a potion healing 5 HP). Click 'Use' to activate or 'Cancel'.
- EMPTY (Grey Bg): Safe tile.
- Goal: Reach the deepest floor possible!
