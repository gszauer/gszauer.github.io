How to Play Boxelot

- Start by clicking tiles with a '?' adjacent (Up/Down/Left/Right) to revealed areas.
- Tiles without symbols ('') or with 'X' are blocked or unreachable.
- MONSTER (Icon on Red Bg): Click revealed monster to attack. Combat is turn-based (Monster may hit first!). Defeat monsters to unblock neighbors & drop a Coin 🪙.
- KEY HOLDER: Attack the monster! A 🗝️ icon appears after first hit. Defeat it to drop the Key Item.
- KEY ITEM (🗝️ on Yellow Bg): Click the dropped key to pick it up.
- DOOR (🔒/🔓 on Brown Bg): Click unlocked door 🔓 with the key 🗝️ to advance (consumes key).
- COIN (🪙 on Yellow Bg): Click revealed/dropped coin to collect 10 Gold.
- HEART (💖 on Pink? Bg): Click revealed/dropped heart to heal 3-5 HP.
- TRAP (Dark Grey Bg): Damages you instantly upon reveal.
- BARREL (🍯 on Brown Bg): Click revealed barrel to break it open (Enemy? Coin? Potion? Heart? Key? Nothing?).
- RANDOM TILE (✨ on Grey Bg): Cycles through items (🍷, 🗡️, 🥦, 💖, 🪙, 🧨). Click to get current or take damage from 🧨.
- ITEM (Icon on Green Bg): Click the revealed item icon (🍷, 🗡️, 🥦) to pick it up.
- INVENTORY (Footer): Click items (max 6). A popup appears showing item details (like 🍷5 for a potion healing 5 HP). Click 'Use' to activate or 'Cancel'.
- EMPTY (Grey Bg): Safe tile.
- Goal: Reach the deepest floor possible!
