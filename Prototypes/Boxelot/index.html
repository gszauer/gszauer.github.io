How to Play Boxelot

The Goal

Descend as deep into the dungeon as possible! Find the key 🗝️ on each floor, unlock the door 🚪, and proceed to the next level.

Basics

  • Reveal Tiles: Click tiles marked with '?' to reveal tiles. Only tiles adjacent to already revealed tiles can be revealed.
  • Unreachable Tiles: Tiles without '?' or 'X' cannot be clicked yet.
  • Blocked Tiles: Tiles marked 'X' are blocked by an adjacent, revealed monster. You must defeat the monster to reveal the tile.
  • Floor Clear Bonus: Defeat all monsters on a floor before opening the door to receive a Gold bonus!

Player & Inventory

  • Stats: Track your Health, Attack, and Gold in the header.
  • Inventory: The footer shows your inventory. Click an item to see details.
  • Using Items: For usable items, click 'Use' in the item detail panel to activate its effect.
  • Selling Items: For 'Durable' items (see below), click 'Sell' in the item detail panel to gain Gold.

Item Drops

These items that have an immediate effect when you interact with them. They are not added to your inventory, their effects just happen.

  • Coin (🪙): Pick up to gain gold.
  • Heart (💖): Pick up to heal.
  • Explosive (🧨): Instantly deals 2 damage to YOU!

Usable Items

These items can be picked up and placed in your inventory. Clicking on an item in the inventory will let you read what it does and use it.

  • Potion (🍷): Use to heal .
  • Blade Scroll (🗡️): Use up to damage all revealed monsters.
  • Poison Vial (🧪): Use to poison all revealed monsters (damage over time).
  • Freeze Scroll (❄️): Use to freeze all revealed monsters (prevents them from attacking).
  • Sheep Scroll (🐑): Use to turn all revealed monsters into weak sheep.

Durable Items

These items provide passive effects while held in your inventory. Click them in the inventory to view details and optionally Sell for Gold.

  • Attack Rune (👊): Grants extra Attack.
  • Defense Rune (✋): Grants extra Maximum HP.
  • Shield (🛡️): Grants some chance to dodge monster attacks.
  • Spike Trap (🪤): Causes monsters to take damage when they are first revealed.
  • Lantern (💡): Reveals the location of the hidden key 🗝️ on every floor.
  • Invisibility Cloak (🧥): Prevents revealed monsters from blocking adjacent hidden tiles.

Monsters

Various monsters appear with increasing HP/ATK on deeper floors. Click a revealed monster to attack it. Combat is turn-based. You attack first, and if the enemy survives they attack back.

Monsters can be Poisoned (🧪) to take damage each turn or Frozen (❄️) - to skip their attack.

General Monsters: General monsters (🕷️🐺👹🐦‍🔥🦇💀🧟👿👺) will only attack you if you attack them. Defeated monsters have a chance to drop a Coin 🪙, and will unblock adjacent tiles. One regular monster on each floor holds the Key Item 🗝️ needed for the door.

Skilled Monsters: Skilled monsters act like regular monsters, but they have special abilities:

  • Thief (🥷): Steals Gold when revealed! Defeat it to get the gold back, plus extra.
  • Assassin (👤): Attacks YOU for 1-6 damage immediately upon being revealed!
  • Dark Healer (🧙‍♂️): Tries to heal other revealed monsters each turn. If none are damaged, it attacks you instead.
  • Necromancer (🧛‍♂️): Low attack, but may summon Skeletons (💀) onto empty revealed tiles each turn (up to a limit).
  • Evil Archer (🧝🏼): Attacks you after *every* action you take while it's revealed (unless frozen)!

Shapeshifters: When these monsters HP reaches 0, they transform into a different form. You must defeat the second form to fully clear them.

  • Shellshifter (🐌->🐚): Basic shapeshifter.
  • Phoenix (🐦‍🔥->🦅): A fiery bird that reforms into an avian.

Bosses: Bosses are much tougher than regular monsters! Defeating a Boss guarantees a Durable item reward or a lot of gold.

  • Orc (🧌), Ghost (👻), Squid (🦑): Powerful bosses with high stats.
  • Lizard (🦕): A shapeshifting boss (see above). The reward is only granted after defeating its second (🐉) form.
  • Master Thief (🦹): Only appears if you possess valuable Durable items. Steals one when revealed! Defeat it to recover your stolen item.

Special Tiles

  • Door (🚪): Starts revealed. Unlocks 🔓 if you have the key. Click the unlocked door to advance to the next floor.
  • Key (🗝️): Appears on the tile after defeating the Key Holder monster. Click the key to pick it up.
  • Barrel (🏺): Click a revealed barrel to break it open. It might contain an item, or not.
  • Random (✨): When revealed, rapidly cycles through potential items (🍷🗡️🧪❄️🐑💖🪙🧨). Click it again to stop the cycle and receive the currently displayed item. Watch out for the Explosive 🧨!
  • Shop (🏪): Appears every few floors. Click to spend Gold 🪙 to increase Max HP, Attack, or fully heal.
  • Challenge (🎯): Appears every few floors. Interacting with it requires meeting certain conditions (like having enough HP ❤️). They may trigger automatically when specific events happen. Completing the challenge rewards a Durable item or Gold.
