How to Play Boxelot

- Click grey tiles ('?') to reveal them.
- MONSTER (Icon on Red Bg): Click again to attack. Combat is turn-based (you hit, monster hits). Defeat monsters for potential loot.
- KEY HOLDER: One monster has a key icon (🗝️). Defeat it to make the Key Item drop.
- KEY ITEM (🗝️ on Yellow Bg): Click the dropped key to pick it up. You need it to open the door.
- DOOR (Brown Bg): Shows locked (🔒) or unlocked (🔓). Click the unlocked door with the key to advance.
- GOLD (Yellow Bg): Click revealed gold to collect.
- TRAP (Dark Grey Bg): Damages you instantly upon reveal.
- ITEM (Icon on Green Bg): Click the revealed item icon (e.g., 🍷, 🗡️) to pick it up.
- INVENTORY (Footer): Click items in your inventory (max 6) to use them.
- EMPTY (Grey Bg): Safe tile.
- Goal: Reach the deepest floor possible!
