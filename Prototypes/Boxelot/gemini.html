Boxelot - How to Play

Objective

Descend as deep as possible through the dungeon floors by finding the key 🗝️ and unlocking the door 🚪 on each level.

Basics

  • The dungeon is a grid of hidden tiles.
  • You start next to the revealed Door 🚪.
  • Click adjacent hidden tiles marked with a '?' to reveal them.
  • Tiles marked with an 'X' are blocked by adjacent monsters and cannot be revealed yet.
  • Tiles with no marker are currently inaccessible.

Tile Types

  • Empty: (Dark Grey) Safe to reveal. Opens up adjacent tiles.
  • Monster (🕷️💀🧟): (Red) Click to attack. Monsters attack back!
    • Stats (❤️HP / 🗡️ATK) appear below the icon when revealed.
    • One monster per floor holds the Key 🗝️. The key icon appears next to the monster *after* you attack it once.
    • Defeating the key monster reveals the Key tile. Other monsters leave behind an Empty tile.
  • Key 🗝️: (Yellow) Click to pick up the key. You need this to open the Door.
  • Door 🚪: (Brown) Starts revealed. Shows 🔒 (locked) or 🔓 (unlocked when you have the key). Click when unlocked 🔓 to proceed to the next floor (consumes the key).
  • Gold 💰: (Gold) Reveals "GOLD +Value". Click to collect.
  • Trap 💥: (Dark Grey) Reveals "TRAP! -Value HP". Damages you immediately!
  • Item (🍷🗡️): (Green) Reveals an item icon. Click to pick it up if you have inventory space.

Combat

  • Click a revealed monster to attack it.
  • You deal damage equal to your ATK 🗡️.
  • If the monster survives, it deals damage equal to its ATK 🗡️ back to you.
  • Your current HP ❤️ and ATK 🗡️ are shown in the top bar.

Inventory & Items

  • Your inventory (max 6 slots) is shown in the bottom bar.
  • Click an item icon in the inventory to see its details and potentially use it.
  • Potion 🍷: Heals a fixed amount of HP.
  • Blade Scroll 🗡️: Damages all revealed, living monsters.
  • Items are consumed on use.

UI

  • Top Bar: Shows HP, ATK, Gold, current Floor, and a 🗝️ icon when you hold the key.
  • [?] Button: Toggles this help panel.
  • [D] Button: Toggles the debug panel (for developers/testing).
  • Bottom Bar: Shows your inventory.

Tips

  • Be careful revealing tiles next to monsters ('X'). Defeat the monster first to unblock them.
  • Manage your HP and use potions wisely.
  • Prioritize finding the key monster.
  • Use Blade Scrolls when multiple enemies are revealed.
