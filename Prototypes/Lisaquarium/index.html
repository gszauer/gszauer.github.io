Insaniquarium Emoji Deluxe

Objective: Buy 3 Egg Pieces (🥚) per level to advance and unlock new pets!

Feeding: Click in the tank to drop food (💧 $5). Guppies (🐠) must eat to grow.

Growth & Coins:
🐠 (Small) -> eats -> 🐡 (Medium) -> drops 💰 ($15)
🐡 (Medium) -> eats -> 🐳 (Large) -> drops 🪙 ($35)

Collection: Click on falling coins/items to collect them before they disappear.

Shop (Top Bar): Use money to buy more Guppies, Food Upgrades (💊, 💧💧), Carnivores (🍖), Weapon Upgrades (💥), or Egg Pieces (🥚).

Aliens: Invaders (👽, 👾) will attack your fish! Click on them with your mouse (laser cursor 🖱️💥) to shoot and defend your tank.

Pets:
🐌 (Stinky): Auto-collects items from the tank bottom.
🦪 (Niko): Produces valuable Pearls (⚪ $100) - click to collect.
⚔️🐟 (Itchy): Helps attack aliens.

Carnivore (🍖 $1000): Eats Small Guppies (🐠) to produce valuable Diamonds (💎 $200).
